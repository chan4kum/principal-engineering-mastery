# Numerical drills

[Module index](../README.md)

Work all 15 variations by hand before checking with NumPy. Label units, dimensions, or conditioning assumptions where they apply.

1. For k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 successes in four Bernoulli trials, find the MLE of p.
2. For k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, find the sample mean of [1,2,k].
3. For k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, find the unbiased sample variance of [−k,0,k].
