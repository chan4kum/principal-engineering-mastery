# M01.06 · Cartesian products

[M01.05](M01.05.md) · Foundation · [M01.07](M01.07.md)

## Problem

Two axes of a grid are paired with zip.

## Mechanism

A Cartesian product includes every ordered pair from the two sets. For finite sets, product size is the product of sizes.
