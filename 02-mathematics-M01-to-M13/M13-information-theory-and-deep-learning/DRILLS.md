# Numerical drills

[Module index](../README.md)

Work all 15 variations by hand before checking with NumPy. Label units, dimensions, or conditioning assumptions where they apply.

1. For target probability p = 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, compute cross entropy in bits.
2. Apply one gradient step to w²/2 from w=2 for η = 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.
3. For x = −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, compute the derivative in w of (wx−1)²/2 at w=1.
