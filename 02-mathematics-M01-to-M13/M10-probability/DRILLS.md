# Numerical drills

[Module index](../README.md)

Work all 15 variations by hand before checking with NumPy. Label units, dimensions, or conditioning assumptions where they apply.

1. For k = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 red items in a 10-item population, compute the probability of red on one draw.
2. For those five populations, find the probability of two reds without replacement.
3. For a Bernoulli variable with p = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, compute its mean and variance.
