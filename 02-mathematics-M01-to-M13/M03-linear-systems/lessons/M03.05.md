# M03.05 · determinants

[M03.04](M03.04.md) · Foundation · [M03.06](M03.06.md)

## Problem

A signed area is mistaken for a sum of row products.

## Mechanism

In 2×2, determinant is ad−bc. It gives oriented area scaling, flips on a row swap, and is zero exactly when the matrix is singular in exact arithmetic.
