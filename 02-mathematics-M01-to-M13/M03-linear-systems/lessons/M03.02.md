# M03.02 · simultaneous equations

[M03.01](M03.01.md) · Foundation · [M03.03](M03.03.md)

## Problem

A candidate solution satisfies one equation and fails the other.

## Mechanism

Write Ax=b and check all residual components. A square full-rank system has a unique exact solution; other systems can be inconsistent or underdetermined.

## Worked example

For equations x+y=5 and x−y=1, add to derive x=3, then y=2. Substitution of (4,1) fails the second row.

Read the [executable calculation](../examples/M03.02.py) after predicting both outcomes.

Expected outputs are `[0, 0]` and `[0, 2]` respectively. Record the changed input, formula or invariant and restore the original control.
