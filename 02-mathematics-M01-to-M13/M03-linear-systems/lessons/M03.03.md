# M03.03 · Gaussian elimination

[M03.02](M03.02.md) · Foundation · [M03.04](M03.04.md)

## Problem

Elimination divides by a zero diagonal entry.

## Mechanism

Maintain an augmented matrix. Swap in a nonzero pivot, eliminate the column below it and back-substitute. Partial pivoting chooses the largest available absolute entry in the current column.
