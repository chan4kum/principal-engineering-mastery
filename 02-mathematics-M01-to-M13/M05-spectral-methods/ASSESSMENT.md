# M05 applied assessment

[Module index](README.md)

Attempt all five cases independently before opening feedback. Award one point per correct result with reasoning. Four points meet 80%. Use changed inputs for a delayed re-test.

1. Give an eigenvector of diag(4,−2) for λ=−2.
2. Find the spectral radius of diag(−0.9,0.1).
3. Give the eigenvalues of [[4,1],[1,4]].
4. Find the second coordinate after three applications of diag(1,−1.5) to (0,2).
5. How many independent eigenvectors does [[2,1],[0,2]] have?

<details>
<summary>Answers and rationale</summary>

1. For example, (0,1).
2. 0.9.
3. 5 and 3.
4. −6.75.
5. Its eigenspace has dimension 1.
