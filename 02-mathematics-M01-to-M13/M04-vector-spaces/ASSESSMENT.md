[Module index](README.md)

Attempt all five cases independently before opening feedback. Award one point per correct result with reasoning. Four points meet 80%. Use changed inputs for a delayed re-test.

1. Find the L1 norm of (−3,4).
2. Compute (2,−3) · (4,1).
3. Are (2,6) and (1,3) independent?
4. Find a nonzero vector orthogonal to (3,4).
5. Give the rank and nullity of [[1,2,0],[0,0,1]].
