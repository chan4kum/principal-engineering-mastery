## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Expectation is the probability-weighted mean; variance is expected squared deviation from that mean. Variance has squared units.

**Trace.** outcomes → weighted mean → squared centered differences.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

Equal outcomes 0 and 4 have mean 2 and variance 4; deterministic 2 has variance 0.
