## 1. The problem & why it exists

An async server's event loop becomes unresponsive during a blocking call. Use async-compatible I/O or offload a bounded blocking operation. A synchronous worker is fine for low concurrency.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

An event loop can run another task when a coroutine suspends. A normal blocking call retains the loop thread until it returns.

**Trace.** A awaits 30 ms -> B awaits 30 ms -> timers overlap. Replace awaits with blocking sleeps -> B starts only after A finishes.

**Boundary case.** Declaring a function async does not make its body cooperative. Measure heartbeat delays and move blocking calls off the loop.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

## Standards and implementation notes

Waiting on an async timer yields the loop. Running a synchronous blocking call inside a coroutine can stall other tasks. Offload suitable blocking I/O to a bounded worker pool.
