## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

The TLB caches translations. A miss can be resolved by a page-table walk. A fault transfers control to the kernel, which may allocate a frame, load backing data or reject access.

**Trace.** Touch page A -> demand-zero minor fault -> translation installed. Touch A again -> translation may hit; no new allocation.

**Boundary case.** Equating TLB misses with disk I/O leads to wrong diagnosis. Compare fault categories and storage observations.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

For bytes B touched once at each page start with page size S, touches are ceil(B/S). 64 KiB and 4 KiB give 16. Reading a new mapping may fault fewer times than touches under read-ahead.
