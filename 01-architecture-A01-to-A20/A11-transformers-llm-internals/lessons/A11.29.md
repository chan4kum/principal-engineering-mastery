## 1. The problem & why it exists

Attention allocates a whole score matrix even though only outputs are needed. Compare memory traffic and output tolerance, and check backend eligibility.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

FlashAttention tiles attention and maintains stable softmax statistics to reduce transfers between memory levels. It computes exact attention up to numerical effects without the naive score materialization.

**Trace.** tile K and V → update running softmax statistics → produce output.
