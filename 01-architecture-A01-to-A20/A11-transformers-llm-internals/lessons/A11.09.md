## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

LayerNorm normalizes over configured feature dimensions and usually applies learned affine parameters. Epsilon stabilizes small variance.

**Trace.** feature vector → mean and variance → normalize → affine.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math
