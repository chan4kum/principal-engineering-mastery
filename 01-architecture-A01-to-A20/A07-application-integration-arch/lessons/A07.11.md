## 1. The problem & why it exists

A service split creates more on-call work with little performance benefit. Estimate operational and data coordination costs along with deployment independence.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Microservices can enable independent releases and scaling. They add remote error semantics, separate lifecycles, data ownership, tracing and operational burden.
