Predict effects for reserve, successful charge, and failed confirmation. Restart the coordinator at the compensating state. The replay applies refund then release and ends cancelled. Next, stop the publisher after delivery and the consumer after applying its effect. Replay both and check that the delivery count exceeds projection effects. The local adapter tables model external participants. Production compensations need separate participant contracts and retries.

Predict the response or state before running. Record normal, broken and recovered evidence and name the boundary that controls the outcome. Scratch variants and pytest data can be discarded after recording evidence. The Redis container is private and self-removing.
