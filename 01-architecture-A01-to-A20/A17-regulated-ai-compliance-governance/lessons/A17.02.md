# A17.02 · compliance vs engineering control

[A17.01](A17.01.md) · Foundation · [A17.03](A17.03.md)

## Problem

An inventory reference is used as evidence of control operation.

## Mechanism

Separate obligation, design control, operating procedure and evidence. Define scope and effectiveness criteria before sampling.
