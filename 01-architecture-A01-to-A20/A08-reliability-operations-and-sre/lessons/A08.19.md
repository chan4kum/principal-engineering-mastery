## 1. The problem & why it exists

An incident team confuses expected data loss and outage duration. Measure both outcomes in a restore exercise and include delayed dependencies.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

RPO bounds acceptable data loss expressed in time. RTO bounds recovery duration. Report observed values against their objectives, including verification and routing time.

**Trace.** last recoverable point → failure → validated service recovery.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

If records are recoverable through 12:40 and incident begins at 12:45, the observed recovery gap is five minutes. A 12:59 recovery takes 14 minutes from the incident boundary.

Predict the worked quantities by hand, then run the [example](../examples/A08.19.py). It uses a small calculation or state model. For arithmetic, retain the units attached to the worked formula.
