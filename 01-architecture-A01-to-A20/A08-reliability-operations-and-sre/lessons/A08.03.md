## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

A reliability budget allocates tolerable user harm. Multiplying independent availability estimates can approximate mandatory serial dependencies, while correlated failures require a different model.

**Trace.** overall objective → dependency assumptions → measure observed path.
