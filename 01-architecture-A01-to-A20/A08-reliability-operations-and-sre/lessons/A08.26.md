## 1. The problem & why it exists

A trace sample misses rare slow requests but dashboards imply none occurred. Measure low-rate failures using complete metrics and choose sampling policy deliberately.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Head sampling selects traces early from available context. Tail sampling uses later evidence and needs buffering and reliable span collection. Sampled traces complement full counters.

**Trace.** all requests → selected traces → late or immediate decision.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

At 10000 requests and uniform 1% sampling, about 100 traces are expected. If ten requests are slow, expected captured slow traces are only 0.1.
