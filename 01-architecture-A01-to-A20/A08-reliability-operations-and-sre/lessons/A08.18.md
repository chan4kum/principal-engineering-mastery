## 1. The problem & why it exists

A backup job succeeds every day but nobody can open the recovered database. Run restores under a measured objective with read-back checks and documented dependencies.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

A backup is useful when independent restoration is compatible, complete and verified. Use a database-aware snapshot or export, preserve required log chains, and test invariants after restore.

**Trace.** backup → restore into disposable target → compare invariants.
