## 1. The problem & why it exists

A GPU workload asks for devices but lands on an unsuitable topology. Measure device memory, topology, queue time and runtime compatibility on target hardware.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Kubernetes exposes devices as resources through supported plugins. Device count does not promise interconnect or locality. Placement and topology need explicit runtime support.

**Trace.** device capability → scheduling claim → placement → benchmark topology.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math
