## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

An image contributes read-only layers and the container has a writable layer. A volume attaches storage with a separate lifecycle. Mounting over a directory can hide its prior contents.

**Trace.** image layers → writable layer or mounted volume.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

If ten replicas each write 50 MiB into separate writable layers, they consume 500 MiB before snapshots and filesystem overhead.
