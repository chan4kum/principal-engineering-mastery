Predict how an old reader competes with a rename for a relation lock. The fixture checks a bounded lock timeout, drains the reader, tries the rename in a transaction, rolls it back, and confirms the old query still works. It then adds a nullable replacement field, backfills it, and runs both old and compatibility readers. Explain what dual-write policy would keep those readers consistent during a rollout. Vary row count and measure a bounded backfill.

Record normal, failed and repaired observations with the statement order or elapsed units. The runner uses a private PostgreSQL container without published ports and removes it and its anonymous volume when it exits. Remove scratch variants after recording the evidence.
