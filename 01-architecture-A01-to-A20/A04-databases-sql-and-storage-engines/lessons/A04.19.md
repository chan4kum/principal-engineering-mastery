## 1. The problem & why it exists

A recent insert causes an OFFSET page to repeat a row. Use stable ordering. If an exact snapshot is required, keyset alone does not supply it.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

OFFSET counts positions at each query. Keyset continuation seeks after a previously returned ordered key. Use a unique tie-breaker and bind cursor policy to the query scope.

**Trace.** read keys 10,20 → insert 15 → seek after 20.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

An offset of 10000 and limit 25 may visit 10025 entries; a suitable index seek can then visit roughly 25 entries after locating the key.
