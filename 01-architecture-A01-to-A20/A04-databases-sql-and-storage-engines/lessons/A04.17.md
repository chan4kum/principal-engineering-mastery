## 1. The problem & why it exists

A replica contains the same accidental deletion as the leader. Measure recovery point and recovery duration in restore drills. Replication is not historical retention by itself.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

A backup provides an independent historical copy. PostgreSQL PITR uses a compatible base backup plus a continuous archived WAL chain to a chosen recovery target.

**Trace.** base backup → archived WAL → restore before destructive commit.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

A backup at 12:00 and contiguous WAL through 12:10 can support targets in that interval; a gap at 12:04 blocks replay beyond the gap.
