## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Multi-hop retrieval follows intermediate entities or evidence relationships. Each intermediate claim needs provenance and scope. Branching increases latency and error risk.

**Trace.** first evidence → intermediate entity → second retrieval → joined evidence.
