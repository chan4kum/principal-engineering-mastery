## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Recall@k counts judged relevant items found. Reciprocal rank emphasizes the first relevant rank. nDCG discounts ranked relevance gains and normalizes by ideal ordering.

**Trace.** ranked candidates → relevance labels → recall, RR and discounted gain.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

First relevant at rank four yields reciprocal rank .25. If two of five relevant items appear in the cutoff, recall is .4.
