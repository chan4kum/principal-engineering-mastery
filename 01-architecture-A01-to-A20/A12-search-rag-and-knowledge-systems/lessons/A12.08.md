## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Precision is retrieved relevance over returned results; recall is recovered relevance over all known relevant items in the judged set.

**Trace.** results and judgments → count relevant hits → compute ratios.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math
