## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

In MCP the host manages integration, a client connects to a server, and servers expose negotiated features including tools, resources or prompts. Discovery is separate from authorization.

**Trace.** host → client session → approved server → enabled capabilities.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

Five advertised tools and two approved for a workflow means only two may execute under its policy.
