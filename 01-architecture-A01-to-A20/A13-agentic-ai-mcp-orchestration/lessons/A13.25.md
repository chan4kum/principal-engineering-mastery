## 1. The problem & why it exists

A workflow running for days loses its durable position during a process restart. Use bounded work units and define pause, cleanup and reconciliation.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Long-running agents need durable state, replay-safe calls, leases or ownership and version migration. External dependencies may expire mid-run.
