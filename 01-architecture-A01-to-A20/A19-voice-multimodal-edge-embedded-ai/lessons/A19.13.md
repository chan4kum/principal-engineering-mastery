# A19.13 · barge-in and interruption

[A19.12](A19.12.md) · Core · [A19.14](A19.14.md)

## Problem

An old turn keeps playing after the user interrupts.

## Mechanism

On deliberate barge-in, cancel the live turn, flush queued audio and ignore late stale chunks by generation ID. Separate acoustic echo from genuine user activity.
