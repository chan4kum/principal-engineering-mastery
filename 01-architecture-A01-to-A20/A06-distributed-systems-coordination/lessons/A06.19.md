## 1. The problem & why it exists

A newly elected leader overwrites a log entry already committed by a prior leader. Do not order candidate logs solely by length. Keep term and vote durable as required.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Raft candidates compare last log term then index. Matching previous index and term is a prerequisite for appending. Committed entries survive valid elections under the protocol.

**Trace.** new term → eligibility vote → append with previous-term check.

**Election.** A follower starts a new election by advancing its term and voting for itself. Each vote is limited per term, and a candidate must satisfy log freshness to get the vote. Receiving a message with a higher term updates authority state.

**Replication.** AppendEntries carries the previous log index and term. A follower rejects a mismatched prefix; the leader backs up or uses conflict information and retries. A follower deletes conflicting suffix entries only as defined by the protocol. Trace how the freshness and quorum rules protect previously committed entries across leader changes.
