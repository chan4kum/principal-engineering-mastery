## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Wall clocks represent calendar time and can jump. Monotonic clocks measure intervals in one observer's time domain. Logical clocks capture ordering constraints without measuring physical duration.

**Trace.** wall time steps backward; monotonic elapsed continues.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

When a wall reading moves from 30 s to 25 s, subtraction says −5 s. Monotonic readings of 9 s and 12 s correctly indicate 3 s elapsed on that observer.
