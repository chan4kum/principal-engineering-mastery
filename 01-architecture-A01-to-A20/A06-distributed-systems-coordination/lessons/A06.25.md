## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Gossip spreads observations over repeated contacts. Membership views can be stale and detectors can disagree. Random fanout is probabilistic and repeated contacts overlap.

**Trace.** local suspicion → one peer → repeated rounds → wider observation.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

In an idealized no-overlap process where each informed node informs one new node, population can grow from 1 to at most 2^r after r rounds. Four rounds yield at most 16.
