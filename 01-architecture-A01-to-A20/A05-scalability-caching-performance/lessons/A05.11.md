## 1. The problem & why it exists

Adding Redis raises miss latency under a poor hit rate. Account for miss overhead and failover load before adopting a cache.

## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

With lookup c=2 ms, backend b=20 ms and hit fraction h=0.9, average path cost is c+(1−h)b=4 ms.
