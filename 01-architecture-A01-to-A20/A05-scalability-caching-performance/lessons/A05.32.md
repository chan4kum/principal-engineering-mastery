## 3. Step-by-step mechanism & minimal dry-run

Include offered, accepted, successful, failed, rejected and timed-out rates. Open-loop load maintains arrivals independently of completions; closed-loop often slows offered load during stalls.

**Trace.** offered load → accepted and rejected → outcomes and latency.

## 4. Formal rigor & quantitative math

If 1000 requests are offered, 800 accepted and 700 successful, the end-to-end success rate is 70%, versus 87.5% among accepted work.

Predict the worked quantities by hand, then run the [example](../examples/A05.32.py). It uses a small calculation or state model. For arithmetic, retain the units attached to the worked formula.
