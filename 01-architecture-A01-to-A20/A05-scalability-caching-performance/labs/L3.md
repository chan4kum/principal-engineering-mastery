The bounded open-loop fixture schedules 48 jobs at fixed planned times, independently of previous completions. Three permits each service a 25 ms wait. Predict the effect of reducing arrival spacing from 12 ms to 1 ms, then record p50, p95, p99 and count. Response duration includes scheduling delay relative to the planned arrival. Repeat each run, change spacing, and identify an envelope that satisfies a chosen tail target. The harness injects waits and has no external dependencies.

Save the normal, failure and repaired measurements. Keep the workload and units fixed apart from the changed input. Remove any scratch variants.
